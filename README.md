# LC Fantasy Cricket App

Clickable prototype of a season-long fantasy cricket league for SA20 Season 5 (2027), modelled on ESPN fantasy football.

**Live demo:** https://yadahbrooks-jpg.github.io/leecoller-fantasy-cricket/

Demo login: username `Lee.test`, password `123` (mock only, no real authentication).

## How it plays
- Opening animation, mock sign-in / sign-up, spinning-ball loader
- 8-team league: snake draft (12 rounds), each player owned by one team only
- Weekly lineup: WK, 2 BAT, 2 AR, 3 BWL, FLEX, plus 3 bench
- One SA20 round per fantasy week; head-to-head matchup against one rival each week
- Free agents: add a player by dropping one
- Regular season weeks 1–7, top 4 to playoffs (two-week semis, one-week final)
- Home dashboard: record, standing, this week's opponent, your lineup on a cricket field

## Data notes
Squads are the SA20 2027 squads after the 7 October 2026 auction (as reported by Wisden). Player roles, projections, fixture order and match results are prototype values generated by the app. Scoring follows the Dream11 T20 points system. Everything is stored in the viewer's browser.
